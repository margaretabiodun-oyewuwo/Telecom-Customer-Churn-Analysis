Telecom Customer Churn Analysis
Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn patterns in a telecom company using Microsoft Excel. The goal of the analysis is to identify key factors influencing customer attrition and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention and reduce churn rates.
Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecommunications industry because retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. This analysis helps uncover customer behaviors, service usage trends, and demographic patterns associated with churn.
Business Problem
The telecom company has been experiencing a high rate of customer cancellations. Management requires data-driven insights to understand:
Why customers are leaving
Which customer groups are more likely to churn
What services influence customer retention
How customer satisfaction and pricing affect churn
The findings from this analysis can support strategic decisions aimed at improving customer loyalty and profitability.

Key Insights
Some major findings from the analysis include:
Customers on month-to-month contracts showed significantly higher churn rates.
Customers with fiber optic internet service were more likely to churn.
Higher monthly charges were associated with increased customer attrition.
Customers with longer tenure were more loyal and less likely to leave.
Electronic check payment users had relatively high churn rates.
Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were proposed:
Improve Long-Term Customer Retention
Introduce incentives for customers to switch from monthly contracts to annual or multi-year subscriptions.
Enhance Customer Support
Improve customer service quality, especially for high-risk customer segments.
Review Pricing Strategies
Evaluate pricing plans for customers with high monthly charges to reduce dissatisfaction.
Strengthen Loyalty Programs
Reward long-term customers through loyalty bonuses, discounts, and personalized offers.
Improve Payment Experience
Encourage customers to adopt more convenient and secure payment methods.
